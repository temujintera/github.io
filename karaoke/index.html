        AirPlay

        Put it on the TV

        Works with Apple TV and AirPlay TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku and others).

        1. Pick your song and add the music.
        2. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring ⧉, and choose your TV.
        3. Turn your phone sideways and tap TV mode. The sound comes out of the TV too.
        4. Tap the screen any time to pause or skip.
        5. Best on iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen and open Karaoke from there. It runs full-screen with no browser bars.
        1. Click Control Center in the menu bar → Screen Mirroring → choose your TV.
        2. For lyrics only on the TV, choose Use As Separate Display, then click TV window in the player, drag that window onto the TV and click it to go full-screen. Your controls stay on the laptop.
        3. Or just mirror your screen and press TV mode.
        4. Keyboard: Space play/pause · ← → skip 5s · [ ] nudge lyrics · Esc exit TV mode.
        1. Chromecast / Google TV: open this page in Chrome → ⋮ menu → Cast… → Cast tab, then press TV mode.
        2. HDMI cable: plug your laptop into the TV and press TV mode.
        3. Smart-TV web browser: open this page on the TV itself and use a YouTube karaoke version.
        Your range

        Fit to your voice

        Male voices

        Female voices

        Your voice is remembered: every song file you load moves into your range automatically. You can still fine-tune with Lower / Higher. Men singing a woman's song (and vice versa) usually sing it an octave apart. The app takes that into account and picks the smallest change.

        Settings

        Vocal removal strength

        Gentle keeps more of the band. Max removes more voice but can thin out drums and bass. Applies to the next song you load.

        YouTube search (optional)

        How to get a free key (2 minutes)
        1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with Google.
        2. Create a project (any name) and press Enable on “YouTube Data API v3”.
        3. Open Credentials → Create credentials → API key, copy it and paste it here.

        The key is stored only in this browser. It gives about 100 searches a day for free.
